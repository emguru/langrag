# Accounts Payable: Three-Way Matching

## Purpose

Three-way matching compares the purchase order, goods receipt, and supplier invoice before payment. This control prevents paying for goods not received or at wrong prices.

## Process steps

1. **Purchase order (PO)** — buyer commits to quantity, unit price, and vendor.
2. **Goods receipt** — warehouse confirms delivery quantity and condition.
3. **Invoice** — vendor bills against the PO; AP matches all three documents.

Discrepancies above tolerance route to procurement or the requester. Automated AP systems flag quantity variances, price mismatches, and duplicate invoices.

## Exceptions

Partial shipments, blanket POs, and service invoices without receipts require configured exception workflows. Three-way matching is standard for direct materials; two-way matching may apply to utilities and subscriptions.
