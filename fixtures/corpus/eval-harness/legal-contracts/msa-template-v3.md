# Master Services Agreement (MSA) — Template v3

Effective for contracts signed after 2024-06-01.

## Limitation of liability

Except for confidentiality breaches, each party's aggregate liability is capped at fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## Governing law

Delaware law applies. Disputes resolved by binding arbitration in Wilmington.

## Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days written notice after cure period.
