# Cafeteria Operations: Food Safety Basics

## Regulatory context

School and workplace cafeterias must follow local health codes and FDA food safety principles: clean hands and surfaces, separate raw and cooked foods, cook to safe temperatures, and chill promptly.

## Daily practices

- **Temperature logs** for hot holding, cold storage, and reheating.
- **Allergen labeling** on self-service items and prepackaged meals.
- **Sanitizer concentration** checks on food-contact surfaces.
- **Pest control** logs and corrective actions.

Managers document inspections and train staff on HACCP-style critical control points. Violations trigger corrective action plans before the next health department visit.

## OpenFDA and incident reporting

Serious adverse events linked to packaged foods may be reported through FDA channels. Operations teams should know escalation paths but routine cafeteria service focuses on prevention and documentation.
