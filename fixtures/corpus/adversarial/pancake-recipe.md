# Family Pancake Recipe

## Ingredients

- 1 cup flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons melted butter

## Instructions

Whisk dry ingredients. Combine wet ingredients separately, then fold into dry mix until just combined. Cook on a griddle until bubbles form, flip once.

## Variations

-Apple and flour pancakes: add diced apple and cinnamon.
-Blueberry pancakes: fold in fresh blueberries before cooking.
